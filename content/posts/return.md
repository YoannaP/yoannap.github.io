I haven’t written in a long time. This is hopefully my return. 

I like to blame this on having too much to work but in reality, there is always space where you can fit stuff in. There is no blame, there is only deprioritisation. 

I would like to prioritise writing again. Motivation: 

1. It makes me think more clearly. Re-seeing your thoughts on paper helps you re-structure your thoughts. 
2. It’s a way of giving back. At it’s current state my writing is most probably not very useful at all, but at some point it might become helpful. Book summaries could help people decide whether to read a book or not. Thoughts around certain subjects might motivate people to connect or explore more in a given area.  
3. Writing also helps me remember what state of mind I was at said date.
