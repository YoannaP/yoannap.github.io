Multiple reasons to write about this. Most women you speak with say they forget the initial few months after having a child. There is a huge amount of strain your body goes through during birth and you don't really expect the recovery part (I didn't at least) to last so long. 
I would say there are step changes in terms of how you improve, once after about 4-5 weeks when most of the physical dislocations tend to settle after a natural birth (of course birth is very individual and this is not a strict rule but just my experience) and then 6-8 weeks when I would say you start getting a routine and "figuring it out" a little bit more. 
This initial part - definitely not holiday-like. 
After that though, yes, there are difficult moments, yes you do sleep less, yes you do have so many chores to get through, but mostly you spend time with the baby and do those things. You have time to listen, read, watch things you are interested in and, god forbid, actually do some work. 
Here is what my day looks like in total hours in general: 
- I sleep around 6 hours 
- I do about 2 or 3 hours of work
- I nurse, send the baby to sleep, play with the baby around 4-7 hours a day depending on his mood and how much help I have 
- I cook for about 1 hour 
- I clean up / tidy for about 1-2 hours 
- I work out and shower for about 1 hour 
- I walk for about 2-3 hours with the baby + listening to books or podcasts
- I watch some things for about 1 hour
- I have lunch and dinner for about 1 hour

This doesn't look like a holiday set of activities so I'm going to go for no, maternity is not like a holiday. 
However ... there are holiday-like activities: the walks for example and the meal times. 

If you have a significant amount of help, time with baby or chores goes way down and then this looks so much more like a holiday. For example, this week I was spending time with family and I had to do no cooking, tidying, cleaning up (just some clothes here and there) and I was able to be out all day on walks. 

Of course, you don't want quality time with baby to go down for the sake of chores especially once they are a little older. 

I expect these are the activities of a full-time mum with no help, excluding the 2-3 hours of work. I could be wrong, this is only my experience. Anyways, it was fun to reflect.
